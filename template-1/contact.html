Quick answers

The questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Send us a message.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver ourselves every Thursday across Bucks County, and ship cold, in insulated boxes, everywhere else in the United States.

How do I change or cancel an order?

Call or email us before Tuesday at 6pm and we'll change or cancel it. After that, local orders are already being packed for Thursday.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes. Pickup in Bucks County is by appointment. Choose pickup at checkout or send us a message and we'll arrange a time.

Do you make corporate or holiday gift boxes?

Yes. Tell us how many boxes, your budget and the date you need them, and we'll put together a selection with handwritten cards.

What if something arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll replace it.
