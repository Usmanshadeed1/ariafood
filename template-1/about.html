A family table, shared with yours.

Aria is a family business in Bucks County with Spanish roots. We bring in the food we grew up with and deliver it to homes and kitchens across the county.

“We grew up where meals brought everyone together. Aria is how we pass that on, not only through the food, but through the way it's meant to be shared.”

The Aria family

What the name means

Aria was born from our family's Spanish roots and a wish to keep that heritage going. The name stands for the three things that matter most to us.

  • FamilyEvery order is packed and delivered by people who know you by name.
  • ContinuityThe same recipes, the same producers and the same standards, year after year.
  • The next generationPassing on the way Spain eats: slowly, generously and around one table.

From Spain to your door

  1. 1

    We choose the producers

    We carry houses like Fermín, Don Bocarte and La Curiosa, chosen because we would serve them at our own table.

  2. 2

    We taste everything

    Nothing goes on the shelf until our family has tried it. If we would not buy it again, we do not sell it.

  3. 3

    We deliver it ourselves

    Every Thursday we drive orders across Bucks County, and we ship cold, in insulated boxes, everywhere else.
