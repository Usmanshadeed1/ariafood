About Spanish charcuterie

Spain cures pork like nowhere else. Jamón is the whole hind leg, salted and hung for one to four years. Chorizo gets its colour and smoky warmth from pimentón de la Vera. Lomo is the cured loin, lean and delicate. Salchichón is seasoned with black pepper instead of paprika, and sobrasada from Mallorca is soft enough to spread.

The finest pieces come from black Ibérico pigs. When they finish on acorns in the oak pastures of the dehesa, the label says bellota, and the fat turns sweet and melts at room temperature.

Serve
At room temperature, sliced thin, with good bread.
Store
Unopened packs in the fridge. Whole pieces in a cool, dry place.
Pair
Fino sherry, a young tempranillo or a cold beer.
