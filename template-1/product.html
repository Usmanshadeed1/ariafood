Description

Ibérico de Bellota is the finest grade of Spanish ham. Black Ibérico pigs roam the oak pastures freely and finish on acorns, and it's this diet and exercise that give the ham its marbling and nutty, moist flavour.

Fermín slices and packs it in La Alberca, so it is ready for the plate the moment you open it.

Serving and storage

Open the pack 20 minutes before serving and let it come to room temperature so the fat turns glossy. Fan the slices on a plate in a single layer.

Keep unopened packs in the fridge and check the date on the pack. Once opened, enjoy within a few days.

Ingredients

Ibérico pork ham, salt. Dry cured. All pork product. Imported from Spain.

Why bellota tastes different

From October to February, Ibérico pigs roam the dehesa, Spain's ancient oak pastures, and eat little but fallen acorns. The fat they build is rich in oleic acid, the same as olive oil, which is why good bellota melts on your tongue.
