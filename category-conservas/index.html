      How long do tins keep?

      Unopened, for years in a cool cupboard. Like wine, many Spanish tins get better with age as the fish settles into the oil.

      Do I need to cook them?

      No. Everything is ready to eat. Open the tin, serve it at room temperature with bread, and use the oil too.

      What is ventresca?

      The belly of the tuna. It's the most tender, richest cut, and the one Spaniards reach for on special occasions.

      How should I serve a tin?

      Straight from the tin or on a small plate, with crusty bread, a few potato chips, lemon or a dash of hot sauce, and a cold drink.

      Do you ship outside Bucks County?

      Yes. We deliver locally every Thursday and ship tins anywhere in the United States.
